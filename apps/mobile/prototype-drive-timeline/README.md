# PROTOTYPE: drives on Timeline, a drive list and drive detail

Throwaway mockups for OPE-391 (map OPE-377). They are never merged.

Open any `.html` file in a browser, or run `./render.sh A.html` to redraw its PNG.

- `A`: drives inside Timeline (dark scrubber band, Day / Drives switch, detail as a sheet)
- `B`: a separate Drives screen from the person card, full-screen detail, car marks on Timeline
- `C`: Timeline's card becomes a day story of stops and drives, with a Drives only filter
- `S`: shared states: the ⋮ menu, the passenger confirm, Passenger drives, delete

Sam picked **A** on 25 Nov 2026, with icons instead of letters for Driving events. The Drives list follows Timeline's device switch, any of the account's devices can delete a drive, and the shared states S1–S5 stay as drawn. The full decision is in the resolution comment on OPE-391.
