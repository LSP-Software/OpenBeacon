# Drives

This context covers trips by vehicle that a phone records and shares with its Groups, and the switch that controls recording.

## Language

**Drive**:
One trip by vehicle, recorded by the device that made it and shared with the Groups that device's account shares location history with. It runs from when the vehicle set off until it stopped for good, arrived at a Place, or the device stopped recording; short stops in traffic or to drop someone off stay inside it. Walking and cycling are never Drives. Buses, trains and taxis can be, until the device marks them as a Passenger drive or deletes them. Each device records its own Drives, so a phone and a tablet in the same car make two.
_Avoid_: Trip, journey, ride, drive record

**Passenger drive**:
A Drive its device marked "I was a passenger". Other members still see the Drive, but not its score or behaviour events.
_Avoid_: Passenger trip, ride, not driving

**Drive detection**:
A device's switch for recording Drives, shown on the phone as "Drive detection". It is on unless the device turns it off, and only that device can change it. While it is off the device records nothing about driving, and other members see "Drive detection is off" where that person's Drives would be.
_Avoid_: Drive tracking, driving mode, crash detection
