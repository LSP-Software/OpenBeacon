## Refactor priorities

1. Before beta, repair local-pause scheduling and destructive-delete ordering. They are small behavioral fixes.
2. Agree one owner for secret persistence/deletion and cancellation. Bound reads without treating timeouts as missing identities; retain late-write cleanup.
3. Move media deletion to a durable cleanup record when addressing the deletion boundary. Keep provider I/O out of locking transactions.
4. After beta, extract a queue-upload runner/policy from tracking.ts while retaining one session coordinator. Remove import cycles by passing narrow callbacks/dependencies where an existing boundary warrants it, not by creating dozens of forwarding files.
5. Share the group selection/projection and query-state conventions. Remove verified dead modules and unused options. No broad rewrite of the backend, native service, encryption package or UI is supported by this review.

## Confidence and exclusions

No new cross-cutting high/critical finding survived beyond MOB-01. Parent independently repeated SR-01's database reproduction. Accepted malicious-server/key-directory and retained-key limits remain explicit product decisions. UI source coverage cannot establish TalkBack, keyboard, OEM background behavior or final screen appearance. Android unit/Robolectric tests ran through the uncached root CI, but no device UI walkthrough or production-provider exercise occurred.
